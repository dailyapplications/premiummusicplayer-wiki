# Privacy Policy — Premium Music Player

**Developer:** Daily Applications. **Contact:** dailyapplications@gmail.com.

## The short version
- The app has **no account and no server of its own**. Your music and videos, playlists, play history and settings stay on your device.
- The developer does **not** collect or receive your files, listening history or personal information.
- The free app shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google and its partners may process some data to show ads, as described below, and only according to your choices.

## What stays on your device
The app reads the **audio and video files on your device** (through Android's media library) to list and play them. Playlists, the play queue, recently played albums, equalizer and sound settings, theme and language choices, and the "ads off for 1 hour" timer are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Files are never uploaded by the app.

## Network use
The app connects to the internet to load ads and Google's consent form, and to open Google Play's rating prompt. The app plays your own files and works fully offline; it does not stream music or download files.

## Permissions
- **Audio and video (media) access** — to find and play your music and videos.
- **Notifications** — to show playback controls while music plays.
- **Internet and network state** — for ads and the consent form.
- **Approximate location (optional)** — only to work out sunrise and sunset for the automatic Day-Night theme. The location is used on your device and is never sent anywhere. If you decline, the theme switches by clock time.
- **Foreground service and wake lock** — to keep music playing when the screen is off.

## Lock screen, car and watch
What is playing (title, artist, album cover) is shared with Android's media controls so it can appear on the lock screen, in notifications, on Android Auto and on a connected watch. This happens on your device and with the car or watch you connect. If you enable the Last.fm option, the title of the playing song is sent to the scrobbler app you installed; the app itself does not contact Last.fm.

## Ads and consent (Google AdMob)
The app displays banner, native, interstitial and rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your region and choices, Google and its ad partners may use identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address (and a coarse location derived from it) and device information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and some US states) the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Ads → Ad privacy settings**. If you decline, you can still use the app. You can also watch a short rewarded video to switch ads off for one hour (Settings → Ads). Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your country). Ads are shown through standard AdMob settings.

## Your rights
Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For data processed by Google, use Google's own tools (for example Ads Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com) and the ad privacy choices inside the app. If you have questions, contact dailyapplications@gmail.com.
